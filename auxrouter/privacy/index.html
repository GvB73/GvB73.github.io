Last updated: 12 November 2026

SupportNederlands

AuxRouter is a macOS menu bar app made by Bart van Geldrop.

What AuxRouter does with audio

AuxRouter uses the macOS system audio capture feature to pick up the audio your Mac plays and send it, in real time, to outputs of your own audio interface. The audio exists only in your Mac's memory for a few milliseconds while it is being routed. It is never recorded, saved to disk, analysed or sent anywhere.

Data collection

AuxRouter does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no network connections.

Settings

Your settings (selected interface, output names, levels, delay, language) are stored only on your Mac, in the app's own preferences. Deleting the app removes them.

Permissions

macOS asks for permission to record system audio. This permission is needed to route the audio and can be withdrawn at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
